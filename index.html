✦ DISTRICT 1: NEON DOWNTOWN & ARCADE ALLEY ✦

Vibe: Dense vertical high-tech streets, Hongyadong multi-tier pagodas, noodle bars, holographic billboards, and the Heart Smasher Arcade / Claw Machine!

Vibe: Luminous waterfront promenade, cyber cruise ship with red lanterns, shimmering water reflections, and the scenic River Pier Bench where sitting down plays Rosa Walton!
